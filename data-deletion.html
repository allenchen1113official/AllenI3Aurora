資料刪除說明

生效日期：2026-11-19

本頁說明如何要求艾倫報報（Allen I³ Aurora）及其社群發文應用程式（PilotOne）刪除與你有關的資料。行使刪除權利完全免費。

先說結論：本站不提供以 Facebook、Instagram、Threads 或 Google 帳號登入，PilotOne 應用程式只用來把艾倫報報自己的內容發布到經營者本人的帳號，不會讀取或保存其他使用者的社群帳號資料。 本站唯一可能保存你的個人資料的地方是電子報訂閱名單（你的 Email 與訂閱偏好）。

一、我們可能持有哪些與你有關的資料

電子報訂閱者Email、訂閱節奏、訂閱／確認／退訂時間、寄送結果
社群平台使用者無。你在 Facebook、Instagram、Threads、YouTube 對艾倫報報的按讚、留言、追蹤由各平台保存，本應用程式不讀取、不保存你的個人帳號資料；僅保存不含個人資料的貼文彙總成效數字
網站訪客無。本站不使用分析或廣告追蹤

二、方法 A：自行退訂並刪除（電子報訂閱者，最快）

  1. 打開任一封艾倫報報電子報，點信件底部的「管理訂閱」。
  2. 在偏好中心按「退訂」，即時停止寄送。
  3. 如需連同保存紀錄一併完整刪除，請接著依方法 B 來信（退訂後預設只保留最少紀錄 2 年，詳見隱私權政策）。

三、方法 B：來信要求刪除（所有人適用）

寄信至：allenchen1113.official@gmail.com

主旨：資料刪除請求（Data Deletion Request）

內容請附：要刪除的訂閱 Email；若與社群平台有關，請附你的 Facebook／Instagram／Threads 使用者名稱或個人檔案連結。為確認是本人提出，請盡量從該訂閱信箱寄出。

處理流程：

  1. 收到後 3 個工作天內回覆確認收件，並提供一組確認編號，供你日後查詢進度。
  2. 於 15 日內完成刪除：自訂閱名單（Google Firebase）移除你的 Email、偏好與所有相關紀錄，並一併刪除郵件寄送紀錄。
  3. 完成後以 Email 通知你。如依法須保留特定資料，會說明保留項目與理由。

備註：已寄出、存在你信箱中的電子報，以及系統備份中於下次輪替前的副本，無法由我們從你的裝置或第三方伺服器上收回。

四、移除應用程式在社群平台上的授權

若你曾在任何平台上看到或授權過艾倫報報相關應用程式，可隨時自行移除：

  • Facebook：設定和隱私 → 設定 → 應用程式和網站 → 找到應用程式 → 移除。也可前往 facebook.com/settings?tab=applications。
  • Instagram：設定 → 帳號管理中心 → 你的資訊和權限 → 應用程式和網站 → 移除。
  • Threads：設定 → 帳號 → 網站權限 → 移除。
  • Google／YouTube：myaccount.google.com/permissions → 選擇應用程式 → 移除存取權。

移除授權後，應用程式即無法再透過該平台存取你的任何資料；如需一併刪除既有資料，請依方法 B 來信。

五、社群平台上的留言

你在艾倫報報粉絲專頁、Instagram、Threads、YouTube 留下的留言，可由你直接在該平台刪除；也可依方法 B 來信，請我從我管理的頁面上移除。

Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 2026-11-19

This page explains how to ask Allen I³ Aurora (艾倫報報) and its social publishing app (PilotOne) to delete data relating to you. Deletion is free of charge.

In short: this site offers no login with Facebook, Instagram, Threads or Google. The PilotOne app is used only to publish Allen I³ Aurora's own content to the operator's own accounts and does not read or store other users' social account data. The only place this site may hold your personal data is the newsletter subscriber list (your email address and subscription preferences).

1. What data we may hold about you

Newsletter subscribersEmail address, frequency preference, sign-up / confirmation / unsubscribe times, delivery results
Social media usersNone. Your likes, comments and follows on Allen I³ Aurora's Facebook, Instagram, Threads and YouTube are stored by those platforms; the app does not read or store your personal account data. Only aggregate post performance figures containing no personal data are kept
Website visitorsNone. The site uses no analytics or advertising trackers

2. Option A: Unsubscribe yourself (newsletter subscribers — fastest)

  1. Open any Allen I³ Aurora newsletter and click "Manage subscription" at the bottom.
  2. Click "Unsubscribe" in the preference center — emails stop immediately.
  3. To have your records fully deleted as well, follow Option B (by default only minimal records are kept for 2 years after unsubscribing — see the Privacy Policy).

3. Option B: Request deletion by email (everyone)

Email: allenchen1113.official@gmail.com

Subject: Data Deletion Request

Please include: the subscribed email address to delete, and — if it concerns a social platform — your Facebook / Instagram / Threads username or profile link. To confirm the request is yours, please send it from the subscribed address where possible.

What happens next:

  1. Within 3 business days you receive an acknowledgement with a confirmation code you can use to check the status.
  2. Within 15 days your email address, preferences and all related records are removed from the subscriber list (Google Firebase), together with delivery logs.
  3. You are notified by email once deletion is complete. If any data must be retained by law, I will tell you what and why.

Note: newsletters already delivered to your inbox, and copies in system backups until their next rotation, cannot be recalled from your devices or third-party servers.

4. Remove app access on social platforms

If you have ever seen or authorized an Allen I³ Aurora app on any platform, you can remove it at any time:

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites → select the app → Remove. Or go to facebook.com/settings?tab=applications.
  • Instagram: Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Apps and websites → Remove.
  • Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions → Remove.
  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions → select the app → Remove access.

Once access is removed, the app can no longer access any of your data through that platform. To delete any existing data as well, email me as in Option B.

5. Comments on social platforms

You can delete your own comments on the Allen I³ Aurora Facebook Page, Instagram, Threads or YouTube directly on that platform, or email me (Option B) to have them removed from pages I manage.
